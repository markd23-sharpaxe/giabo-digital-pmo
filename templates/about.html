Our Philosophy: Human-in-Control

The era of the AI 'copilot' that merely summarizes meeting notes is over. Intelligence is a commodity; deterministic execution is the differentiator.

At Rapid Viable Product AI, we believe enterprise project managers need systems that respect professional constraints, not just smarter chat windows. What We Do

Digital Employees & AI Agents

We build custom, deterministic AI swarms utilizing our proprietary GIARO framework (Goals, Inputs, Actions, Reasoning, Outputs). These agents route chaotic corporate data into hardened, standardized PMO artifacts like RAID logs and status reports.

AI Project Management Consultancy

We partner with enterprise PMOs to audit existing workflows and implement pragmatic AI strategies that unblock team capacity and enforce objective project outcomes.

Keynotes & Industry Talks

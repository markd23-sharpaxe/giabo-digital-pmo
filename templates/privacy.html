Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rapid Viable Product LTD ("Rapid Viable Product", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the GIABO Digital PMO Swarm (the "Service"), a Microsoft Teams-native project-governance application distributed via the Microsoft Azure Marketplace.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the Service:

  • Subscription & organization data -- the Azure subscription identifier, Microsoft Entra organization identifier, organization name, and selected plan (Free 14-Day Trial or Pro Plan), provided by Microsoft when you activate the Service on the Azure Marketplace.
  • Project data -- project names, locked plans, SharePoint site identifiers, and the document content the Service is granted access to via Microsoft Graph, strictly limited to the permissions your administrator consents to.
  • Conversation content -- messages sent to the digital employee in Microsoft Teams, processed to extract task progress, risks, blockers, and change requests.
  • Usage & billing telemetry -- usage of the digital employee per request, used solely to enforce your plan's usage allowance and for Azure Marketplace metering.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively to: provision and operate your subscription; route your messages to the correct specialist agent and write the resulting decisions back to your project record; detect and prevent abuse of the Service's usage allowances; and provide support when requested. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your project or conversation content to train any model outside the scope of serving your own requests.

3. Sub-processors

Delivering the Service requires processing data through the following Microsoft sub-processors, each operating under its own Microsoft data-processing terms:

  • Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service -- processes conversation content to produce structured agent outputs (task updates, risk assessments, routing decisions).
  • Microsoft Graph / SharePoint -- read access to the document libraries your administrator explicitly grants consent to.
  • Microsoft Bot Framework / Microsoft Teams -- transport for every message exchanged with the digital employee.
  • Microsoft Azure Marketplace / Partner Center -- subscription fulfillment, billing, and metering.

4. Data Storage & Location

Organization, project, and audit-trail data is stored in an Azure Database for PostgreSQL instance operated by Rapid Viable Product. Data residency follows the Azure region in which that instance is deployed; contact us if your organization has a specific residency requirement.

5. Data Retention

We retain subscription and project data for as long as your subscription is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to satisfy accounting, audit, and legal obligations. You may request deletion of your organization's data by contacting us; some records (for example billing history) may be retained where required by law.

6. Security

Access to the Service's Microsoft Graph permissions and Azure OpenAI resources is scoped to your organization. All connections use TLS in transit; stored data is accessed over an encrypted session.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your organization's data. Requests should be directed to your organization's administrator, who can escalate to us via the contact details below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is a business-to-business product intended for use by working professionals within a licensed organization and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to privacy@rapidviableproduct.com.

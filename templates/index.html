The concept

Not a chatbot with a longer prompt. A Digital PMO employee: specialists for schedule, blockers, risk, and change control, working as one office across Copilot and Teams.

Stand the office up from the Azure Marketplace. Point it at a SharePoint site. Talk to it in Microsoft 365 Copilot or Microsoft Teams the way you would brief a coordinator. GIABO routes the request, writes the fact to the project record, and asks the Project Manager before any locked baseline moves. It does not replace the PM. It will not approve its own change.

Core capabilities

The work the office does every day, without a daily blast or a silent rewrite.

S

SharePoint ingestion

New documents and meeting notes in the linked library are brought into the project record automatically. Content is addressed by SHA-256 so the same file is an update, not a second copy — a sovereign audit hash for every ingested artefact.

T

Teams conversation

Log hours, raise a blocker, flag a risk, or request a plan change in ordinary language. The conversation guide hands each message to one specialist. A proposed change pauses as an approval card for the Project Manager.

C

Considerate chasing

Follow-ups are scored by critical path, risk, and deadline — not a morning mail merge. If someone was contacted in the last 24 hours, GIABO stays quiet. Fatigue protection holds even on a critical-path slip.

A

Sovereign audit hashing

SharePoint intake and deterministic office records are keyed with SHA-256. Re-running a sweep does not invent a second artefact. The audit trail stays addressable, idempotent, and yours.

Governance frameworks

PRINCE2, PMP, and Agile intelligence in one office — each specialist stays in its lane.

P2

PRINCE2

Stage tolerances are classified, not bargained. A real breach raises an exception hold until a person acknowledges it or escalates to the board. In-tolerance risk is logged, not dramatised.

PM

PMP

Hours and percent complete stay on the schedule specialist. Critical-path integrity is recorded, not rewritten into a new end date. Moving a locked baseline is change control — never a progress note.

Ag

Agile

Impediments and cross-team waits go to a servant-leader facilitator. You get a blocker and a next step. Progress percent is not silently rewritten, and the locked plan is not slipped to “clear the board.”
